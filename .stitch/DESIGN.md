## Brand & Style

This design system powers a data-driven enterprise resource and decision-support platform designed specifically for Indonesian UMKM (Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah) business owners. The brand ethos is grounded in three core pillars: **Ketenangan** (calm confidence through clarity), **Kemandirian** (empowering independent business decisions), and **Gotong Royong** (approachable, communal growth).

The visual aesthetic synthesizes contemporary B2B SaaS utility with warm, earthy Indonesian material culture. It steers clear of superficial folk motifs or ornate batik tropes, opting instead for structural, organic dignity inspired by tropical botanical canopies, volcanic fertile clays, and warm unbleached woven textiles. 

The emotional response must balance institutional bank-grade security with the welcoming familiarity of a trusted neighborhood partner (*teman usaha*). Every screen must feel immediately intuitive, decluttering complex cash-flow modeling into bite-sized actionable steps for non-technical entrepreneurs.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly for high readability under varied screen conditions—from bright outdoor street kiosks to low-light back-office desks. 

- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Deep Forest Green):** The commanding anchor color. Communicates long-term stability, wealth preservation, and institutional trust. Used for persistent primary navigation, primary CTA actions, and strong visual anchors.
- **Secondary (`#40916C` - Natural Muted Sage):** Balances data density. Deployed for secondary controls, metric trend lines, successful baseline states, and active interactive elements.
- **Accent / Alert (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Clay):** Drawn from fired Indonesian terracotta tile. Acts as the functional accent for priority action calls, attention-requiring highlights, and high-impact micro-interactions.
- **Warning / Highlight (`#D97706` - Warm Amber):** Reserved for cash-flow warnings, pending reconciliation tasks, and threshold alerts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Cream):** Replaces harsh cool whites with an unbleached linen tone, reducing ocular fatigue during extended bookkeeping sessions.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast crisp white containers nested over the warm cream canvas, bounded by subtle warm stone borders (`#EBE6DD`).
- **Typography Charcoal (`#1F2421`):** A softened black with a natural green-gray undertone ensuring AA/AAA accessible reading contrast without the harsh optical glare of pitch black. Secondary details rest in Warm Slate (`#525A55`).

## Typography

This design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a typeface born from modern Indonesian civic identity. It combines geometric clarity with humanist warmth, providing high legibility on lower-dpi mobile displays common across merchant devices.

- **Monetary Formats:** Indonesian Rupiah amounts (`Rp xx.xxx.xxx`) utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) and bold weight variants (`display-currency`, `title-md`) to ensure aligned vertical scanning within ledger tables and aggregate summary cards.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large display headings are reserved for high-level performance overviews and empty states. Analytical metrics and operational dashboards favor `headline-sm` down to `label-md` to preserve informational density without clutter.
- **Localization Tone:** Microcopy and instructional notes avoid technical banking jargon; they prioritize plain Indonesian business terms (*Omzet*, *Pengeluaran*, *Laba Bersih*, *Sisa Kas*).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture implements a disciplined fluid grid engineered for responsive transitions between mobile cashier viewpoints, tablet ordering stations, and desktop accounting dashboards.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `1rem` gutter. Side navigation collapses into a persistent bottom sheet navigation bar or drawer.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer canvas margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Collapsible slim sidebar icon rail.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with a fixed `260px` left-hand functional navigation panel, `2rem` outer canvas margin, and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas limits out at `1440px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking lengths on widescreen monitors.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical rhythm adheres to an 8px base grid (governed by tokens `space-xs` through `space-xl`). Compact inline data displays compress with `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px), whereas distinct metric cards, analytical modules, and simulation panels maintain clear breathing room using `space-lg` (24px) gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating plastic realism and aggressive multi-tier drop shadows. Visual depth is established through a grounded, tactile stacking model inspired by structured stationery and clean architectural joinery:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` (Warm Cream). Non-interactive structural plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` pure white fill with a permanent 1px solid perimeter boundary in Warm Stone (`#EBE6DD`). Ambient resting shadow is ultra-soft and organic: `0 1px 3px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Filters):** Retains the 1px boundary (`#D8D1C5`) and elevates with a warm forest-tinted shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.06), 0 10px 20px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Panels, Simulation Drawers):** Solid white surface, anchored by a deep backdrop scrim tinted with `#143D2B` at 40% opacity with a subtle 4px backdrop blur. Elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework utilizes balanced, human-friendly rounding (Value 2). Radii provide approachable warmth without appearing frivolous or toy-like:

- **Standard Base Radii (8px / `0.5rem`):** Buttons, text input fields, table cell selection wraps, and status badges.
- **Container Radii (16px / `1rem`):** KPI metric cards, analytical chart wraps, contextual alert callouts, and floating quick-action blocks.
- **Prominent Surfaces & Dialogs (24px / `1.5rem`):** Modal windows, bottom sheets on mobile viewports, and primary simulation canvases (*CobaDulu*).
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Status indicator tags, transaction categories, and filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1B4332`, Text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. On hover: `#143D2B`. Pressed: subtle scale transition (0.98).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px `#40916C`, Text `#1B4332`. Hover: background tinted with `#40916C` at 8% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless, Text `#1B4332`. Hover: `#F2EFE9`.
- **Accent Action:** Background `#C85A32`, Text `#FFFFFF`. Used strictly for conversion or urgent recommendations (e.g., "Ambil Tindakan", "Simpan Prediksi").

### KPI Metric Cards
- White surface, 16px radius, 1px `#EBE6DD` border.
- Card Header: Label-sm in `#525A55` paired with a categorical micro-icon in muted sage.
- Metric Display: `headline-md` or `display-currency` in `#1F2421`.
- Trend Badge: Nested inline pill container. Green variant (`#E8F5E9` background with `#2D6A4F` text for positive movement) or Terracotta variant (`#FDF2ED` background with `#C85A32` text for cash outflows or risk). Includes directional micro-arrow.

### Actionable Insights Badges & Cards
- Contextual warning and growth recommendation items.
- Border-left accent: 4px solid strip in Terracotta (`#C85A32`) for urgent business actions or Sage (`#40916C`) for optimization tips.
- Background: `#FAF8F3`, padding `1rem`.

### Data Tables & Status Tags
- Headers: `#F4F0E8` background, 11px uppercase bold labels in `#525A55`.
- Rows: `#FFFFFF` surface, alternating border-bottom 1px `#F0ECE4`. Row hover state: `#FAF8F5`.
- Status Tags:
  - *Lunas / Sukses:* `#E8F5E9` background, `#2D6A4F` text.
  - *Tertunda / Perlu Verifikasi:* `#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text.
  - *Kritis / Jatuh Tempo:* `#FEE2E2` background, `#991B1B` text.

### Interactive Simulation Sliders (*CobaDulu*)
- Track: 6px height, rounded full, background `#E2DCD2`.
- Active Progress Fill: `#40916C`.
- Thumb: 24px diameter circle, `#FFFFFF` with `#1B4332` 2px border, elevated with Level 2 shadow.
- Live Value Callout: Dynamic tooltip displaying simulated outcome with immediate recalculation feedback.

### Progress Rings & Visual Visualizations
- Background ring: `#EBE6DD`.
- Active stroke: Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) transitioning to Natural Sage (`#40916C`).
- Center typography: Bold metric display centered with contextual label beneath.
- Line charts: Smooth monotone splines with dual-tone fills (gradient from `#40916C` at 20% opacity fading to 0% at zero-axis).
